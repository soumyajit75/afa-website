The Flagship Destination

AMUZI CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

Where India meets the world. This is the destination every young player aspires to reach.

Training Facility
What It Represents

FULL-TIME. FULLY FUNDED. WORLD CLASS.

At the top of the pathway sits the Centre of Excellence — a full-time environment covering coaching, sports science, education, nutrition and psychology, with international exposure built in.

Players here don't just train in India. They tour, compete and are tested against elite young talent abroad — the same way Argentina's own academies have operated for generations.

Inside the Centre

WHAT PLAYERS EXPERIENCE

AFA-Certified Coaching

Coaching staff trained and certified against AFA methodology, sourced and supervised at our flagship Centre of Excellence standard.

Sports Science & Psychology

Physical conditioning, nutrition and mental performance support built around each player's development plan.

AI-Assisted Performance Analysis

Match and training data captured and analysed, feeding individual player report cards and development plans.

Education, Alongside Training

A structured academic track runs in parallel with full-time football, protecting every player's future beyond the game.

International Pathway

Tours, camps and exchanges across South America, Africa, Europe and North America — testing Centre of Excellence players against international-standard opposition.

A Direct Scouting Pipeline

The final stage before professional trials — in India's growing leagues, and, long-term, clubs abroad.
