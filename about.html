Who We Are

A STRUCTURED ANSWER TO A DECADES-OLD QUESTION

India fills stadiums and floods television screens every World Cup, yet has given the world almost no footballers of its own. The talent was never missing — the system to find it was. Amuzi Centre of Excellence exists to build that system.

Our Purpose

DISCOVERY, AS AN OPERATING SYSTEM

Somewhere in India right now — on a ground in Bengal, on a pitch in the Northeast, in a narrow alley in Kerala — there is a child who moves the ball like they were born knowing something the rest of us are still learning. No scout has their name in a notebook. No club has a file on them.

Amuzi Centre of Excellence was built to close that gap — connecting the grassroots ocean of talent playing football in Indian feeder centres and clubs every day to a structured pathway capable of turning a child into a professional, and a professional into an international name.

AFA methodology in practice — Argentina National Team training
How We're Built

A PYRAMID, BUILT FOR INDIAN SCALE

Every great football nation is structured like a pyramid. Ours is designed specifically for India.

1. Feeder Centres — Local Clubs & Community

The widest part of the pyramid. Every child who touches a ball here is a possibility.

2. Intermediate Centres — Where Promise Becomes a Pathway

The most promising children rise here, often receiving a formal development contract for the first time — a signal that says: we see you, and we are investing in you.

3. The Centre of Excellence — Where India Meets the World

Full-time, fully-funded, world-class in every sense: coaching, sports science, education, nutrition, psychology — and international exposure through tours and exchanges.

4. The Gateway to Europe
Beti Khilao — Vision 2031

A dedicated pillar using football to build confidence, discipline and community leadership — working toward Vision 2031 and the FIFA Women's World Cup. Pathways for players and future professionals alike.

Discovery Technology

Our broader ecosystem — including OTT & streaming platforms and a Game Management System — is designed to surface talent from every corner of the country and turn training into evidence, not guesswork.
