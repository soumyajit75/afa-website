What The Association Means For Us

METHODOLOGY, NOT JUST A LOGO

Coach Certification

Coaches operating inside AFA-affiliated centres are trained and certified through AFA methodology before working with children.

Tactical & Psychological Curriculum

A holistic curriculum — tactical intelligence, sports psychology and mental resilience — built on Argentine coaching methodology that trains players to decide under pressure.

AI-Driven Talent Identification

Scouting shifts from instinct alone to a digital platform analysing match video and building athlete performance profiles — merit over geography or connections.

International Pathway

Structured exposure through training camps and exchanges across South America, Africa, Europe and North America — testing Indian players against international standards.

Performance-Based Sponsorship

Selected players receive full or partial sponsorship based on their level of performance — removing cost as a barrier to development.

Player Management

We represent the player's interests off the pitch too — contracts, welfare and career guidance handled with the same care as their football development.
