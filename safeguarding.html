Every Child, Protected First

SAFEGUARDING

Amuzi Centre of Excellence works with children and young athletes across every stage of the pathway. Protecting them comes before performance, always.

Full policy in development — summary principles below

Our operating principles already require that every coach working inside an AFA-affiliated centre complete AFA methodology training before coaching children. Our full safeguarding policy — covering reporting procedures, background checks, welfare officers and incident response — is being finalised alongside our network rollout.

Our Commitments

  • Certified coaches only — no coach works with children without completing AFA methodology training
  • Clear, confidential reporting channels for any welfare concern
  • Age-appropriate training loads and supervision at every stage of the pathway
  • Dedicated attention to the welfare of girls in the Beti Khilao programme
  • Data protection for all player records, especially those relating to minors

Report a Concern

If you have a safeguarding concern regarding any programme, contact us directly at info@amuzi.in. All concerns are treated seriously and confidentially.
