Training Facility
What It Represents

FULL-TIME. FULLY FUNDED. WORLD CLASS.

At the top of the pathway sits the Centre of Excellence — a full-time environment covering coaching, sports science, education, nutrition and psychology, with international exposure built in.

Players here don't just train in India. They tour, compete and are tested against elite young talent abroad — the same way Argentina's own academies have operated for generations.
